Privacy Policy

AI Helper — Highlight to Thread
Last updated: October 2, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the AI Helper — Highlight to Thread Chrome extension (“the Extension”) handles information.

Summary

  • The Extension does not have its own backend server.
  • It does not sell your data or show ads.
  • Follow-up questions are answered through your existing logged-in session on ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini — the same way those sites normally work in your browser.
  • Thread history is stored locally in your browser with Chrome’s storage API.

Information the Extension stores

On your device, the Extension may store highlight threads for conversations you use it on, including:

  • quoted / highlighted text,
  • your follow-up questions and the assistant replies in that thread,
  • technical IDs needed to continue a side chat (for example conversation or message identifiers used by the host site).

This data stays in your browser profile via chrome.storage.local. It is not uploaded to an AI Helper server, because there isn’t one.

Information sent to third parties

When you send a follow-up from the sidebar, the Extension uses the page’s normal network requests to the site you are on:

  • chatgpt.com
  • claude.ai
  • gemini.google.com

Those requests include the highlighted snippet, surrounding context the Extension gathers from the visible chat, and your question, so the host site can generate a reply. How those companies handle that information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

The Extension only requests access to those three sites (plus local storage). It does not read your browsing history elsewhere.

Permissions

  • storage — save and restore highlight threads on your device.
  • Host access to ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini — inject the highlight UI and send follow-ups through your logged-in session on those pages.

Children

The Extension is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will be revised. Continued use of the Extension after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project repository at github.com/Ethan7778/ai-helper.

This page is intended for the Chrome Web Store privacy-policy requirement.
